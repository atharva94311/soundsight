    SoundSight

    A modular visual & haptic alert system for hard-of-hearing users

    This is a working 3D twin of the system: a hostel flat with three listening modules, a bedside alert beacon, a bed shaker, a wearable band and a phone app. Trigger a sound and watch it travel — microphone → TinyML on the ESP32 → MQTT → decision engine → light, vibration and text.
